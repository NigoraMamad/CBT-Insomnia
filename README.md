# 💤 Sortie – Your Sleep Coaching

Sortie is a sleep coaching app designed to help people rebuild healthy sleep habits and overcome insomnia — with the help of a friendly 3D mascot and a thoughtful, research-driven experience.

Available for **iPhone** and **Apple Watch**, Sortie is based on **Sleep Restriction Therapy (SRT)** — a structured behavioral method used to improve sleep efficiency by creating consistency and resetting the body’s internal clock.

At the center of the experience is **Sortie**, a 3D character who supports users with daily guidance, motivational reminders, and gentle nudges — turning a difficult journey into a more approachable one.

---

## 🌙 Features

- 📊 **Personalized Sleep Tracking**  
  Tailored insights and sleep analysis that adapt to user progress.

- 🛏️ **Dynamic Bedtime Windows**  
  Structured sleep scheduling that evolves with your sleep efficiency.

- 🚶 **WalkToWake**  
  A unique feature that encourages movement upon waking to promote active mornings.

- 💬 **Sortie the 3D Mascot**  
  A lovable guide that offers encouragement and keeps you engaged throughout your journey.

- ⌚ **Cross-Device Syncing**  
  Smooth integration between iPhone and Apple Watch for a continuous experience.

---

## 🧠 Scientific Foundation

Sortie’s methodology is grounded in **behavioral sleep science**, focusing on long-term habit change rather than quick fixes. Sleep Restriction Therapy has been shown to effectively improve sleep efficiency and reduce insomnia over time.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To begin your journey with Sortie:

1. Download the app from TestFlight: https://testflight.apple.com/join/aPZK9PzU
2. Set up your personalized sleep schedule.
3. Follow Sortie's daily coaching and bedtime prompts.
4. Complete your WalkToWake challenges in the morning.
5. Track your sleep improvements over time.
